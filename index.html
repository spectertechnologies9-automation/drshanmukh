About Dr. Shanmukh Bankapur

Dr. Shanmukh Bankapur is the Founder and Chief Orthodontist of Care Dental Hospital, Bengaluru, with over 25 years of clinical experience in orthodontics and comprehensive dental care. He is known for a patient-first, ethical approach: preserving natural teeth wherever possible and recommending only treatment that genuinely benefits long-term oral health, through personalised plans and modern orthodontic techniques for children and adults.
